Cambrian

539 to 487 million years ago

Most of the main groups of animals alive today first appear in rocks of this age: an explosion of new bodies, with eyes, legs, shells and spines. All of it happens in the sea. The land is bare. You dive 508 million years back, to the foot of a reef wall. Nothing alive here is longer than your arm. Shapes follow the fossils. Light, colours and behaviour are guesses.
