Devonian

420 to 359 million years ago

The age of fishes. Fish now have jaws. Some wear armour of bone and grow as long as a car, and the first sharks swim beside them. On land the first forests grow, and near the end the first animals with four legs leave the water. You dive about 360 million years back, into the open sea over what is now Ohio. The floor below has no air.
