Ordovician

487 to 443 million years ago

The sea rises higher than ever before and covers most of the continents. Life in it multiplies: sea lilies, corals, moss animals, and hunters in shells as long as a car. The land is still almost bare. You dive about 450 million years back, to the sea over North America. This world ends in an ice age that wipes out most kinds of animals.
