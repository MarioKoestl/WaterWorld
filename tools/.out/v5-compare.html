Bright line across the mouth floor.
In v4 the back of the mouth was a flat wall of quads connecting the last palate ring to the last floor ring (line 156: m.quad(C.pal[k], C.pal[k+1], C.flo[MP-k-1], C.flo[MP-k])). This wall joined two parts that move differently (roof with head aux −0.25, floor with jaw aux −0.40). When the mouth opens, the two halves rotate apart. The seam and the flat geometry could catch a specular highlight as a thin bright line across the floor.

In v5 that flat wall is replaced by a throat tunnel: 18 rings from the corner of the mouth (s ≈ 0.088) back to the gills (s = 0.185), narrowing, with 4 gill arches. The roof (aux −0.25) and floor (aux −0.40) are separate smooth half-tunnels that join smoothly. The geometric cause of the bright line is eliminated.

Additionally the shader now colours the inside as flesh (not black), so any small highlight would blend with the flesh tone. Cannot confirm visually from turntable angles (all views from above or from 24 m). Check in-engine from the front at mouth level.
