# Horn coral (Grewingkia canadensis)

Sources: Digital Atlas of Ordovician Life, "Grewingkia canadensis" (checked):
"reaching lengths in excess of 13 cm", straight or slightly curved, strongly ringed, 100+ walls.

## Form
- Straight or slightly curved horn of calcite, solitary (not colonial)
- Length: up to 13 cm, three sizes in mesh (7–13 cm)
- Opening radius: ~18% of length (~1.3–2.3 cm diameter at opening)
- Strong growth rings along exterior, spaced ~3–5 mm
- Over 100 thin radial walls (septa) inside the cup
- A single polyp with tentacles sat in the opening (soft parts not known)

## Figures used in the mesh
- Length: rr(0.07, 0.13) m
- Opening radius: length × 0.18
- Tip taper: power 0.65 from tip to opening
- Curve: 2–10% of length (slight arch)
- Ring bumps: amplitude 2 mm, frequency ~55 per unit length
- Tentacles: 8, length 7–14 mm, radius 1.2 mm (estimate)

## What stays an estimate
- Tentacle number, length and shape (soft parts unknown)
- Exact taper profile
- Ring spacing and amplitude
- Cup interior depth
- All colours
