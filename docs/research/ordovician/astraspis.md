# Astraspis (early armoured fish)

Sources:
- Wikipedia "Astraspis" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astraspis): about 200 mm, 8 gill openings each side,
  large eyes, oval cross-section.
- Sansom and others 1997 (abstract only;
  https://palass.org/publications/palaeontology-journal/archive/40/3/article_pp625-643):
  dorsal headshield of polygonal tesserae with star-shaped or smooth bumps; Harding Sandstone, Colorado.
- Brief says to read Sansom 1997 for the tail; abstract checked 2026-11-23.

## Form
- About 200 mm long, like a large tadpole
- Oval in cross-section, wider than tall
- Head and chest under a shield of polygonal tesserae (small bony plates, each > 2 mm)
- Tesserae have star-shaped or smooth bumps on their surface
- 8 gill openings on each side, behind the shield, slanting down and back
- Large eyes at the sides of the head
- Tail made up of large rhomboid scales (Sansom 1997 abstract); tail termination unknown
- No other fins, no jaws

## Figures used in the mesh (all in body lengths, 1 unit = one animal)
- Body: 12-section loft, oval cross-section (1.08× wider than tall)
- Widest point: at about 60% from tail end (the shield region)

- Gills: 8 per side, 0.025 apart along z, tubes of radius 0.003

## What stays an estimate
- Every width and cross-section proportion (no source gives a cross-section)
- Tail shape beyond "scaly" (Sansom 1997 not fully accessible)
- Caudal fin shape and size
- Gill opening size and exact placement
- Eye size
- All colours (grey-brown assumed, like limestone armour)
- Mouth placement and size (not modelled)
