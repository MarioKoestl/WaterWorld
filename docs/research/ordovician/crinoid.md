# Crinoid (sea lily)

Sources: Digital Atlas of Ordovician Life (not verified).

## Form
- Stalk of stacked round discs (columnals), a cup (calyx) on top, 5 feathery arms
- Height: 30 cm to 1 m (three variants from seed)
- Stalk: 65–70% of total height
- Cup: 8–10% of height
- Arms: 20–25% of height, spread ~2× cup diameter

## Figures used in the mesh
- Stalk radius: 6–10 mm (scales with height)
- Cup radius: 2.8× stalk radius
- Disc ridges: amplitude 1.2 mm, frequency ~160 per metre along stalk
- 5 arms, each tapering from 28% of cup radius to near zero
- Arms curve outward and slightly upward, then droop (forming a bowl)
- Holdfast at base: 2.2× stalk radius, tapering over 15 mm

## Proportions (in body lengths)
- Stalk: 0.69 of height
- Cup: 0.09 of height
- Arms: 0.22 of height

## What stays an estimate
- Stalk and cup radius relative to height
- Disc spacing and prominence
- Arm spread, droop and taper
- Holdfast size and shape
- All colours (pale calcite assumed)
