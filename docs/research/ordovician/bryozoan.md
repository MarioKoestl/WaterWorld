# Bryozoan (moss animal colony)

Sources: Digital Atlas of Ordovician Life (not verified).

## Form
- Branching stony twigs, 5 to 15 cm high
- Each branch ~3–5 mm diameter, tapering to ~2 mm at tips
- 2–3 main stems from a shared base, each branching once
- Surface covered with pinprick pores (zooid apertures), <1 mm each

## Figures used in the mesh
- Height: 6 to 14 cm (rr range), three variants from seed
- Branch radius: 2.8 mm base, tapering with 0.78× at each fork
- Branch depth: 1 level (main stem + one fork)
- Number of main stems: 2 or 3

## What stays an estimate
- Exact branching angles and curvature
- Branch taper rate
- All colours (grey-brown assumed, like limestone)
