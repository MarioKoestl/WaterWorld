# Brachiopod (lamp shell)

Sources: Digital Atlas of Ordovician Life (not verified).

## Rafinesquina (type 0)
- Concavo-convex: pedicle valve (lower) convex, brachial valve (upper) nearly flat or concave
- Hinge line wider than the body, D-shaped outline
- Width ~3.5 cm, length ~3.2 cm
- Upper valve height ~3 mm, lower valve height ~4 mm (estimate)
- Surface: faint growth lines, no strong ribs
- Colour: not known from fossils; cream to grey-brown assumed

## Platystrophia (type 1)
- Biconvex: both valves convex
- Strong radial ribs (plicae), ~14 across the shell
- Width ~3 cm, length ~2.4 cm
- Upper valve height ~5 mm, lower valve height ~6 mm (estimate from reconstruction)
- Hinge line narrower than Rafinesquina

## Dead shells
- Single valves scattered on the floor, hollow side up or down at random
- Same mesh as Rafinesquina upper valve (more common shape)

## What stays an estimate
- Heights of the valves (cross-section not measured on a figure)
- Exact proportions of width to length
- All colours
