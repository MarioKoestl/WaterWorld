# Small orthocones (Treptoceras)

Sources: Digital Atlas of Ordovician Life (not verified here); brief specifies "darker bands on a pale shell, on the
upper side only" and "a little wider than high".

## Form
- A straight conical shell, smooth outside, slightly oval cross-section (wider than tall)
- Shell length: about 65% of total body length
- Opening diameter: about 30% of shell length
- Soft parts emerge from the opening: a simple head with eyes and short arms (all estimate)
- Length: 15 to 40 cm (whole animal including tentacles)

## Figures used in the mesh
- Modeled one unit long from shell tip (z=0) to tentacle tips (z≈0.85)
- Shell: z=0 to z=0.55, tapering from radius 0.075 at opening to 0.004 at tip
- Cross-section: 1.08× wider than tall
- Inner lip at the shell opening: 3 inset rings
- Head: z=0.55 to z=0.72, loft tapering from opening to head front
- Eyes: balls at z=0.60, radius 0.012
- Arms: 8 short tentacles from head front, length 0.06
- Funnel (hyponome): under the head, for jet propulsion (estimate)

## Colour (from brief)
- Darker bands on the upper side only (dorsal; local y > 0 in mesh space)
- Underside plain and pale
- Tentacles pinkish-brown (estimate, as in living nautilus)

## Middle forms (after Nautilus, the living model)

1. **Hood** — tough leathery wrinkled flap over the top of the head, shaped to close the opening; about 8% of body length
2. **Eye swelling** — round bulge on each side under the hood, with a small dark pinhole (no lens); about 4% of body size
3. **Funnel** — soft tube under the head for jet propulsion, pulses when swimming; about 6% of body length
4. **Tentacle sheaths** — thicker bases from which 35 tentacles emerge, arranged around the mouth; each about 3% of body length
5. **Rim of the shell opening** — flared lip with visible thickness, one looks into dark interior past it; about 5% of opening diameter
6. **Body bulge** — where the soft mantle swells over the shell rim, filling the opening; about 3% of shell radius
7. **Growth undulations** — subtle radius variations along the shell parallel to the opening; about 1% of local radius

## What stays an estimate
- Soft parts entirely (head, eyes, arms, funnel): no soft tissue known
- Shell thickness and inner surface
- Cross-section shape (oval assumed from related genera)
- All colours except the dorsal bands (brief specifies those)
