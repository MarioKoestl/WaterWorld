# Endoceras (Endoceras giganteum)

Sources: Wikipedia "Endoceras" (checked 2026-11-16); Wikipedia "Endocerida" (checked 2026-11-16).

## Form
- A straight conical shell with low annulations (rings) around it
- Shell length up to about 5.73 m (a 2015 estimate from a 3 m preserved specimen at the Museum of Comparative Zoology)
- A report of 9.1 m was never confirmed
- Endoceras has annulations; Cameroceras, found in the same Cincinnati rocks, is smooth
- Opening diameter ~0.3 m (estimate from brief)
- At the wide end: a head with eyes and a ring of arms (soft parts entirely unknown)
- Probably the top predator of the Ordovician, hunting close to the sea floor

## Figures used in the mesh
- Modelled one unit long from shell tip (z=0) to arm tips (z≈0.88)
- Shell: z=0 to z=0.72, tapering from radius 0.026 at opening to 0.001 at tip
- Cross-section: 1.06× wider than tall (like orthocones, slightly oval)
- Annulations: low ring bumps with amplitude ~0.001 units, period ~0.012 along z
- Inner lip at the shell opening: 3 inset rings
- Head: z=0.72 to z=0.80, loft tapering from opening to head front
- Eyes: at z=0.74, radius 0.009 (large but proportionally small)
- Arms: 10 tentacles from head front, length ~0.08, using curve()+tube()
- Funnel (hyponome): under the head

## Middle forms (after Nautilus, the living model)

1. **Hood** — tough leathery wrinkled flap over the top of the head, shaped to close the opening; about 6% of body length
2. **Eye swelling** — round bulge on each side under the hood, with a small dark pinhole (no lens); about 2% of body size
3. **Funnel** — soft tube under the head for propulsion, steady glide; about 4% of body length
4. **Tentacle sheaths** — thicker bases from which 38 tentacles emerge, arranged around the mouth; each about 2% of body length
5. **Rim of the shell opening** — flared lip with visible thickness, dark interior; about 5% of opening diameter
6. **Body bulge** — soft mantle swelling over the shell rim; about 3% of shell radius
7. **Annulations** — low ring bumps running around the shell, visible as geometric relief; about 3-4% of local radius
8. **Growth lines** — finer lines between annulations, parallel to the opening; about 0.5% of local radius

## What stays an estimate
- Soft parts entirely (head, eyes, arms, funnel): no soft tissue known
- Shell thickness and inner surface
- Cross-section shape
- Opening diameter (0.3 m)
- Annulation spacing and amplitude
- All colours
