# Concavicaris: sources and numbers

Notes for `docs/briefs/concavicaris-build.md`. Read on 2026-11-19. L is the length of the shell, from its front tip to
its rear edge.

The species is C. submarinus of Morocco, of the same age as the Cleveland Shale; the thylacocephalans of the Cleveland
Shale itself were not looked up.

## What they say

- F2, caption: a 3D reconstruction of the holotype PIMUZ 37349 from the front, from behind, from above and from the
  side; the shell yellow, the body purple, the eyes blue, "the paddle-like limbs in white"; scale bar 10 mm.
- F1, caption: the holotype with "the ventral curve" and a depression from the rear end until midway through the
  shell; the gills, putative gastric muscles and a paired structure inside.
- F5, caption: the arms for catching are reconstructed "with a size and shape intermediate" between older and younger
  forms; it is shown preying on a conodont, without direct evidence that it did.
- `devonian-models.md` (from the text of JHK2020): the best specimen 56 mm long, 26 mm high, 15 mm wide; specimens
  from 27 to about 100 mm; highest in front of the middle; the front tip a point with a notch for the eye under it; the
  rear end cut off and gently rounded; a groove along the lower edge; the surface smooth but for a fine net of
  many-sided cells; eyes about a seventh of the length of the body, with six-sided lenses; eight pairs of small paddles
  at the lower rear end; leaf-shaped gills inside; not a fast swimmer, in open water over a floor without oxygen.

## Not known
